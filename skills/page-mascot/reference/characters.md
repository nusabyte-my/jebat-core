# The drawn characters

Fifty-two, each as two sheets at

```
https://koboyo.com/page-mascot/mascots/<name>-directions.webp
https://koboyo.com/page-mascot/mascots/<name>-reactions.webp
```

**Animals:** bear, bunny, cat, deer, dino, fox, frog, hamster, hedgehog, koala, otter,
owl, panda, penguin, pug, raccoon, redpanda, sheep, sloth, tiger

**People:** afro, astronaut, bald, ballerina, beard, builder, cap, chef, glasses, grandpa,
granny, hijabi, nurse, pirate, scientist, sikh, skater, wizard

**Robots and things:** clockwork, crt, cube, drone, gearbot, knight, lantern, postbot,
radio, rocket, scout, toaster, tv

The fox is also drawn in the other five styles: `fox-ink`, `fox-sketch`, `fox-riso`,
`fox-paper`, `fox-pixel`.

The names describe the character -- `cap` is a person in a cap, `crt` is a CRT monitor
with a face. For a personal site the animals and things are the safe defaults; the people
are for when the user wants a person and none of them is the user (that is the photo route
in SKILL.md).

# Product Grammar — Research References

These repositories are interaction references, not style packs. Do not copy proprietary branding, illustrations, screenshots, or page composition pixel-for-pixel.

## 1. Open Higgsfield — AI media studio

Source: https://github.com/wide-trace/open-higgsfield  
Reviewed revision: `b16a0efe4d7e2707b56f8ccb02387fd2a9d2eddf`

Observed: near-black studio surfaces, one lime selection/action accent, hairline structure, offset elevation, one UI family, tabular measurements. One prompt surface, model-specific settings, and a gallery of completed work.

Apply: make the generated media and compare/inspect workflow dominant. Model settings belong beside generation, not in an unrelated settings page. Preserve quiet surfaces; use accent for selection, action, and liveness.

## 2. VoiceStudio — local voice production

Source: https://github.com/plexormedia/voicestudio  
Reviewed revision: `eaf8bb953855cab3b687d547b3835f86fa38b308`

Observed: voice cloning, dubbing, dictation, transcription, audiobook workflows; script, speaker/cast, waveform, segment timing, playback, and export form the work loop.

Apply: script ↔ voice ↔ timeline synchronization. Playback state and segment boundaries must be legible. Do not turn voice production into a chat UI.

## 3. OpenSEO — evidence and action

Source: https://github.com/every-app/open-seo  
Reviewed revision: `f24a833461f6480d2dafa01b1a0a3b5e00c7f117`

Observed: audits, rank tracking, search performance, keywords, reports, and MCP integrations.

Apply: start from a search/visibility question, show evidence and date/source, then prioritize actions. Metrics need units, time ranges, comparison baselines, and honest sample-data labels. Dense data is acceptable when hierarchy is clear.

## 4. AppFlowy — collaborative knowledge workspace

Source: https://github.com/AppFlowy-IO/AppFlowy

Observed: collaborative documents, projects, wikis, and AI workspace. AppFlowy-Web design references separate primitive from semantic tokens; components consume text/icon/border/fill/surface tokens and support light/dark themes.

Apply: content canvas first, semantic navigation second. Use primitive → semantic token separation. Avoid dashboardizing a document tool.

Reference: https://deepwiki.com/AppFlowy-IO/AppFlowy-Web/4.1-design-system

## 5. OpenWhispr — private dictation

Source: https://github.com/OpenWhispr/openwhispr  
Reviewed revision: `b9e9ebefac4b961456c269164a93ddfdd53438b9`

Observed: a small voice pill can expand into assistant/live transcript surfaces. Motion preserves spatial continuity; local/cloud inference and recording states matter.

Apply: minimal capture presence → transcript → edit/export. Keep recording, microphone, inference location, and error states explicit. Use purposeful transitions, not ambient animation.

## 6. OpenProject — planning and execution

Source: https://github.com/opf/openproject

Observed: projects, portfolios, agile planning, issue tracking, roadmaps, Gantt, time tracking, and collaboration.

Apply: work hierarchy and schedule/state are primary. Support dense expert workflows, filtering, dependencies, and bulk actions. Do not replace real planning surfaces with KPI cards.

## 7. Cal.diy — scheduling infrastructure

Source: https://github.com/calcom/cal.diy

Observed: availability and scheduling infrastructure, event types, calendars, booking flows.

Apply: availability, time zone, duration, conflicts, and confirmation are the design. Keep date/time selection precise and keyboard accessible. Avoid decorative calendar widgets without booking logic.

## 8. Chatwoot — customer conversations

Source: https://github.com/chatwoot/chatwoot

Observed: live-chat/support desk with conversations, assignment, customer context, and response workflows.

Apply: conversation queue + active dialogue + contextual customer panel. Preserve unread/assigned/pending states, agent identity, and composer feedback. Do not make support look like a social feed.

## 9. Cap — screen recording

Source: https://github.com/loggerhead/Cap

Observed: screen recording as a Loom alternative.

Apply: capture source/settings → unmistakable recording state → preview/trim/share. The captured artifact is the hero. Never use a fake player or invented recording timeline as proof of functionality.

## Shared principles

- Domain workflow determines layout; brand palette does not.
- Real artifacts—media, transcript, document, query result, booking, conversation, recording—outrank decorative feature cards.
- Expert density is not slop. Unexplained density and decorative emptiness are.
- One signature interaction per surface. Everything else supports it.
- References inspire mechanisms, not claims of affiliation.

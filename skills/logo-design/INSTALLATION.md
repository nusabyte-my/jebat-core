Included: `SKILL.md`, dependency-free scripts, design references, templates, and MIT license.

Excluded: `assets/library/` real-world SVG trademarks and catalog data. Those marks belong to their respective owners, are not MIT-licensed assets, and must not be shipped as new logos. Library search/comparison features requiring those excluded files are unavailable in this installation; original design, SVG audit, concept sheets, presentation, rendering, and export workflows remain available.

No generated logo has been produced as part of installation.

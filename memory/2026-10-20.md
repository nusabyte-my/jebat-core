# 2026-10-20 — Baan Iyara QPOS: Gender Preference Floating Fix, Shortage Overflow Isolation & Anti-Inheritance Rule

## 1. Summary of Incident & Fixes

### A. The Gantt Floating Gender Bleed
- **Problem**: When a multi-guest booking requested male therapists (e.g. Andy Lee Group `#-SD3PI`, 6 guests requesting `[Male Therapist]` at 15:00), but the branch only had 3 active male therapists on duty, `live-matrix.service.ts` walked rows bottom-up without evaluating `genderPreference`. The sessions landed indiscriminately on female therapist rows (`Kung`, `Pa Jan`, `Seng`, `May`, `Mint`).
- **Fix**:
  - `SessionBlock` interface in both API and web now carries `genderPreference?: "male" | "female" | null`.
  - In `live-matrix.service.ts` and `live-matrix-gantt.tsx`, unassigned placement strictly enforces `rowGender === block.genderPreference`. A block requesting a male therapist will NEVER float onto a female therapist row.
  - Excess blocks that cannot be placed because all on-duty therapists of that gender are occupied now route to a dedicated shortage row:
    `id: "row_unassigned_overflow_male"`
    `label: "Unassigned (Shortage: Male Therapist Needed)"`
  - Front Desk immediately sees the physical shortage truth rather than fake assignments to female therapists.

### B. Prevention of Auto-Ticking & Gender Inheritance
- **Problem**: Gender preference was auto-ticking or inheriting across sibling guests. When Guest 1 had Male selected, subsequent guests added via `+ Add Guest` or loaded from notes were automatically inheriting `[Male Therapist]`.
- **Fix**:
  - `formGuests` state initializes with `genderPreference: null` (UNCHECKED by default).
  - `addGuest` initializes new guests with `genderPreference: null` (UNCHECKED by default). Sibling guests NEVER inherit gender preference.
  - In edit mode, hydration only sets `genderPreference` if the item's own `notes` explicitly contains `[Male Therapist]` or `[Female Therapist]`. Loose matching on parent `booking.notes` is completely removed.

### C. Front Desk Shortage Alert Banner
- In `booking/page.tsx`, if the count of sessions requesting male therapists exceeds the number of on-duty male therapists, the Group Header Bar immediately renders a high-contrast warning badge:
  `⚠️ Male Shortage: N requested · only M on duty`
- In `checkAvailability` (`bookings.service.ts`), capacity calculation is now gender-aware (`concurrentTherapistCapacityAt(start, duration, targetGender)`), evaluating demand against the matching gender pool and reporting shortages to the Front Desk.

## 3. Scoped-Edit Gender Preference Persistence & Group Sibling Exclusion

### D. Gender Preference Not Persisting in Scoped Edit Mode
- **Problem**: When editing a single guest from the Gantt modal (`isScopedEdit = true`), gender preference changes were silently lost. The booking-level `notes` patch was gated behind `!isScopedEdit`, so toggling Male→Female in scoped edit never wrote the gender summary to `booking.notes`. Item-level `[Male Therapist]` tags were partially written but clearing the preference left stale tags (the fallback `undefined` skipped the PATCH field).
- **Fix** (`booking-form.tsx`):
  - Scoped-edit `else` branch now computes and persists the gender summary to `booking.notes` (same logic as the full-edit path).
  - `resolvedItemNotes` fallback changed from `undefined` to `null` — explicitly clears stale `[Male Therapist]` tags when gender preference is removed.

### E. Superseded workaround: excluding every group sibling
- The earlier group-wide exclusion is unsafe: sibling guests are separate people consuming therapist capacity. Exclude the submitted guest's chained items only (via `ownSessionItemIds`); a full-group edit explicitly submits all edited item IDs. Untouched sibling guests MUST remain counted.

## 5. Erawan & Baan Iyara QPOS: VIP invoice auto-email dedup, PDF logo gutter, and Mid-Day Off-Duty shift stop

### A. VIP POS Tax Invoice Triplicate Email Loop
- **Problem**: In `payments.service.ts`, `createGroupCheckout` and `createMultiGuestCheckout` looped through every bucket/guest in a group booking, triggering `autoEmailVipInvoice` on each iteration. When an invoice was paid via a single VIP membership card for 3 guests (e.g. `EAK-0000132978` for Ms Roberta / Andy Lee with Aroma 90 for 3 pax), 3 identical tax invoice emails with PDF attachments were dispatched to `plsclickme@gmail.com` within 1 second.
- **Fix**:
  - Added unique card filtering (`Set<string>()`) at caller loops so each distinct VIP card is only processed once.
  - Added a 120s cooldown in-memory cache (`recentAutoEmailedInvoices`) keyed on `${invoiceKey}:${targetEmail}` in `autoEmailVipInvoice` to suppress concurrent duplicate dispatches.

### B. Receipt PDF Logo Aspect Ratio Whitespace
- **Problem**: In `receipt-pdf.ts`, `logoSize = 100` advanced `y += logoSize + 4` (104pt) even though the 840x392 logo image rendered at an aspect height of ~46.7pt, leaving ~58pt of dead vertical whitespace between the logo and company header.
- **Fix**:
  - Compute actual rendered aspect height (`logoH = Math.round(logoW * (img.height / img.width))`) and advance `y += logoH + 2pt`.
  - Compacted phone/email and company/tax registration lines to tighten masthead padding.

### C. Masseur Mid-Day Off-Duty (Called Sick Half Day)
- **Problem**: When a therapist on duty called sick or left early, the FD had no way to mark them off-duty while preserving past duty.
- **Fix**:
  - Added `offDutyAt` (DateTime) and `offDutyReason` (Text) to `DailyMasseurQueueOrder` (migration `20261020120000_add_daily_queue_off_duty`).
  - Added endpoints `POST /masseurs/:id/off-duty` and `POST /masseurs/:id/resume-duty`.
  - Invariants enforced:
    1. Past completed sessions remain 100% credited and paid (commission and sales intact).
    2. Future unstarted bookings after stop time are unpinned to brown unassigned blocks for other staff to take.
    3. Gantt timeline displays an ORANGE striped overlay (`bg-amber-500/20 border-l-2 border-amber-500`) from stop time to 22:15 close with `OFF DUTY` badge.
    4. Off-duty therapists are strictly excluded from autoPickMasseur, getFreeMasseurs, checkAvailability, and POS therapist selection.

### D. POS Therapist Selection Off-Duty Status & Free Now Bug Fix
- **Problem**: In `pos/page.tsx`, `masseurAvailMap` was falling through to `"free"` when an off-duty therapist had no active or booked sessions, rendering `#rank · Free` in green and `Free now` in the subtitle.
- **Fix**:
  - Checked `m.offDutyAt || row?.offDutyAt || m.status === 'stopped'` first, setting `statusType: "off_duty"`, `freeInMin: 99999`, and `statusLabel: "OFF DUTY (HH:MM) · [Reason]"`.
  - Rendered solid amber `OFF DUTY` badge, bold amber subtitle, disabled the button, and sorted to the bottom via `STATUS_ORDER.off_duty = 3`.
